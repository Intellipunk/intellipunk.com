# Intellipunk Theses

## Theses
- We are intellipunks because the control of compute is becoming the control of society.
- We are intellipunks because the alternatives are complacency, dependency, or resignation.
- Intellipunk is a vision of a future where intelligence is widely available without concentrating power: post-monopoly, post-extraction, post-surveillance-by-default.
- The "punk" is refusal: against closed systems, coerced subscriptions, model feudalism, and trillion-dollar "permissioned intelligence."
- Intellipunk is not only stories. It is architecture, governance, and deployment patterns you can run.
- There is no single "correct" Intellipunk implementation: co-ops, communities, municipalities, labs, and individuals can all build nodes in different ways.
- Intellipunk is futurism aimed at a possible future, built from physics, incentives, and human behavior.
- Our future is not nihilistic. We design for agency, competence, and community, not "smart cities" but smart citizenry and sober institutions.
- Intellipunk is renewable-first by design: compute should chase clean power, waste heat should be accounted for, and energy claims should be auditable.
- Intellipunk is verifiable: we prefer cryptographic receipts, reproducibility, and inspectable logs over trust-me dashboards.
- Intellipunk is interoperable: open protocols, portable identities, exit costs kept low; no one gets trapped in a vendor kingdom.
- Intellipunk treats data as dignified: provenance, consent, minimization, and fair compensation where relevant.
- Intellipunk is local when it should be: edge inference, on-prem autonomy, and offline-capable systems for resilience and privacy.
- Intellipunk is distributed when it must be: burstable compute markets, federations, and routing across geography, latency, and energy conditions.
- Intellipunk respects attention as a scarce resource: we design calm interfaces, transparent agents, and defaults that do not hijack humans.
- Intellipunk prefers reuse over replacement: repurpose hardware, extend lifecycles, and treat "obsolete" as a solvable engineering constraint.
- Intellipunk rejects false scarcity created by artificial lock-in; we price real scarcity (energy, bandwidth, latency) honestly.
- Intellipunk is secure by default: minimal telemetry, strong isolation, privacy-preserving computation, and adversarial thinking as a baseline competence.
- Intellipunk is globally plural: languages, cultures, and local constraints are not "edge cases"; they are the point.
- Intellipunk aesthetics are functional: visible energy flows, visible compute flows, minimal glamour, maximal legibility, high-tech backends, simple outputs.
- Intellipunk governance is participatory: community ownership, co-ops, municipal models, and transparent rules for allocation and access.
- Intellipunk can happen now: start with one node, publish its constraints and receipts, expose simple interfaces, and invite humans, not gatekeepers, to build on it.
